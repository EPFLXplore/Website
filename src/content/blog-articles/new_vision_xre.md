Xplore Research (XRE), the third pole of EPFL Xplore, is entering a new chapter. What began with a set of predefined projects, and we are now moving to an open platform for **student-led innovation** in space robotics. This article lays out where we come from, what changes starting September 2025, and how you can be part of it.

## Where we come from

Between **2023 and 2025**, Xplore Research launched advanced projects that pushed our team into complex robotics systems:

- **Chienpanzé** : a prototype of a legged robot exploring alternative locomotion.
- **WALL-E** : a system for recycling plastic into printable filament.
- **AI Division** : exploring artificial intelligence for the European Rover Challenge and beyond.

These projects proved that Xplore can innovate well beyond the competition rover.

## What changes now

Starting **September 2025**, Xplore Research is opening its doors to **student-led projects** related to space robotics, the field that designs robotic systems to explore and operate in space environments.

Any EPFL **Bachelor's or Master's student** can submit an idea, form a team, and compete for access to our **labs, resources, and funding**.

## Why this evolution?

Xplore Research started as a place for predefined projects, but it now evolves into a platform for **your ideas**. By empowering students to propose, design, and lead their own work, XRE becomes a space for independent thinking, interdisciplinarity, and genuine innovation in space robotics.

## The proposal

Turning an idea into a working prototype follows four steps:

1. **Proposal** : form a team and submit your idea.
2. **Review** : Xplore evaluates the feasibility of your project.
3. **Prototype** : design and build it with our support.
4. **Demonstration** : present your final results.

## Get involved

Your idea could be the next leap in space robotics. Download the proposal template, put together your team, and submit your project.
